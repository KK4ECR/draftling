### OPENING

- ##### Three years ago. Proud of a full calendar.
- ##### Back to back meetings. Phone buzzing.
- ##### “Slammed.” Said like a badge.

### THE QUESTION

- ##### One night: what did I finish this year?
- ##### No answer. Not in ten seconds, not in ten minutes.
- ##### Couldn’t name five things.

### THE DIFFERENCE

- ##### Busy measures motion. Productive measures progress.
- ##### Motion looks good. Gets compliments. “I don’t know how you do it all.”
- ##### Motion never asks where you’re going.
- ##### Progress asks: closer to the life you want? The real one, not the performed one.

### THE COST

- ##### Missed my daughter’s recital.
- ##### Picked a status meeting. Should’ve been an email.
- ##### Busy hides behind a full schedule. Calls itself responsibility.

### 168 HOURS

- ##### Same for everyone. Billionaire, grocery bagger, you, me.
- ##### Busy fills all 168 with motion.
- ##### Productive spends some on purpose.

### THE TOOL

- ##### Every night: “What did I finish today that mattered?”
- ##### Not attended. Not responded to. Finished.
- ##### Fast answer: productive day, even if quiet.
- ##### Scrolling for an answer: busy day.
- ##### One week. Seven honest answers. No app. No planner.
- ##### Small counts. Real talk with my son.
- ##### Small and true beats big and empty.
- ##### No answer stings. Good. Free feedback.

### CLOSE

- ##### Not here to say work less or work more.
- ##### Full calendar is not a full life.
- ##### Wrong test for years.
- ##### 168 hours. Not one more.
- ##### You’ll fill them. Everyone does.
- ##### End of the week: name five things.
- ##### I couldn’t once. Now I do.
- ##### Ask yourself today. Find out which one you are.
